Our Story

Designed in Manama,
worn everywhere.

N Designs began as one woman's answer to a simple gap: modest wear that felt as considered as anything on the international runways she loved. Every abaya, jalabiya and dress we make starts from the same question — does it move well, does it last, and does it feel like you?

Today the collections travel from our studio in Bahrain to wardrobes across the Gulf, Europe and beyond, each piece cut, fitted and finished by hand in small batches.

01

Considered Fabric

We work with brushed crepes, linen blends and matte satins chosen for how they move and travel, not just how they photograph.

02

Small Batches

Every collection is produced in limited runs, cut and finished by hand in our Manama studio — never mass-produced.

03

Made to Travel

Packed and shipped from Bahrain to the Gulf and internationally, with the same care whether you're two streets or two continents away.
